01 / About

Building useful intelligence from complex interactions.

I am affiliated with Renmin University of China, where my current work centers on information retrieval and recommendation.

My research explores real-world recommendation settings in which content, context, and user feedback change over time. Recent projects study live streaming recommendation, room-level collaboration, multimodal content, and the relationship between implicit behavior and explicit preference.

I am interested in research that connects careful modeling with open, reusable datasets and practical systems.
